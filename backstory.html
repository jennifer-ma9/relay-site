Why I built Relay

Written by Jennifer Ma, founder of Relay

When I was a freshman in high school, I joined a club at my school where we would tutor girls in South Africa. Every Wednesday morning, we'd join a Zoom call with 10–14 girls who shared one computer, and work through math and science problems with them. This became one of my favorite clubs, and I hoped to eventually become a head of the organization.

However, the next year, the club founder and Senior Head graduated, and we lost contact with the South African school. I was confused by this, feeling that the students still needed tutors, and the lesson plans still existed, yet the club disappeared because there was no one to take it over.

I've since watched similar things happen to other student-led projects.

Relay is an attempt to make sure a founder graduating doesn't have to mean a project ending.
