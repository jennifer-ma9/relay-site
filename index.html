A second life for good ideas.

Too many student-led projects end when the students who started them graduate. Relay helps those projects find new leaders and gives them the information they need to keep going.

Find a project Pass on a project

Good work shouldn't have to start over.

There are students with ideas. There are students with experience. And there are communities already being served by projects that may disappear when their founders leave. Instead of starting from scratch, what if the next student could pick up where the last one left off?

How it works

How a handoff works

Relay doesn't touch anything legal — your nonprofit's paperwork, bank account, and board stay exactly where they are. We're not trying to transfer the organization itself. We're trying to transfer the knowledge that usually lives in the founder's head.

01

Tell us how it works

Share your project's purpose, schedule, contacts, resources, and everything else a new leader would need to know.

02

Find a new leader

Students looking for a project can browse the ones that are ready for someone new to take them on.

03

Make the handoff

The founder and new leader work together before the transition, so the knowledge and relationships don't disappear along with the founder.
