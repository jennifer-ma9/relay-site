Relay the Idea, Keep the Torch Alive

Too many student-led projects end when the students who started them graduate. Relay helps those projects find new leaders and gives them the information they need to keep going.

Pass the Torch

Somewhere, someone is starting what someone else just left behind.

Every year, students graduate and leave behind projects they've poured years into. At the same time, new students are dreaming up ideas to serve their communities, solve problems, and make a difference. Relay connects the two.

How it works

Every handoff needs two things.

Something worth carrying forward

A project's mission, resources, relationships, and foundation.

Someone ready to carry it

A new leader with ideas, vision, and passion for what comes next.
